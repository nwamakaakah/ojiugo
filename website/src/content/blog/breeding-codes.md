other apps turn birding into a spreadsheet of ticks and tallies. ọjịugo is a diary - a place for the moment, the light, the way your heart lifted when it landed. you keep the story, not just the species count

## so what exactly are breeding codes?

breeding codes are a standardized shorthand system used by ornithologists and birding databases. codes like NY (nest with young), CF (carrying food), NB (nest building), FL (recently fledged young), and S (singing male on territory). they help researchers track bird breeding activity across regions.

> a messy entry beats a perfect one you never wrote.

## why ọjịugo skips them

most casual birders don't need breeding codes - they add friction to what should be a joyful, reflective experience. ọjịugo is built for the feeling of birding, not the paperwork.

## your words, your way

in ọjịugo you write things like 'it was carrying a twig' or 'the chick was still wobbly on the branch' or 'two parents taking turns at the nest'. no codes to memorize, no dropdowns to navigate. just your story.

the best field notes are the ones that bring you back to the moment.
